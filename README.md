## What it does

Stop calculating cut heights in your head. Measure your log at two points,
pick the planks you want, and the planner does the rest:

- **Two diameters in, a full cut plan out.** The app extrapolates the
  taper, picks an optimal layout, and tells you exactly what to crank
  the saw to for every cut.
- **Step-by-step sawing.** A live cross-section illustration shows the
  next cut, how to rotate the log, and what gets produced. Undo and
  redo any time.
- **Multiple optimisation strategies.** Strict priority, value, minimum
  waste, quartersawn-friendly, heartwood-avoid, and an experimental
  2D bin-pack ensemble for the tightest yields.
- **Handles the awkward stuff.** Curved logs, taper compensation, wane
  tolerance for home-grade work, edging guidance for the second pass.
- **Phone, tablet, desktop.** Works on whichever you have at the mill.
  An old tablet propped up by the saw is the typical setup.
- **Offline. No account. No tracking.** Your plans, settings and log
  history live in your browser, nowhere else.

## Try it

Open <https://cullberg.github.io/sawmill-app/> on your phone, tablet,
or computer. The app loads once over the internet, then runs entirely
offline — no connection needed at the mill.

To install as a full-screen app:

- **Android (Chrome):** menu → *Add to Home screen*
- **iPad / iPhone (Safari):** share → *Add to Home Screen*
- **Desktop (Chrome / Edge):** install icon in the address bar

On first launch the app asks for a license key. Trial keys are
available on request — see *Get in touch* below.

## Who it's built for

Made for, and tested on, a real Bamse chainsaw mill in northern
Sweden. Should work on any single-blade mill — Logosol, Norwood,
Granberg, generic chainsaw mills, small bandsaw mills. The
experimental bin-pack strategy comes into its own on edger setups
where you're not bound to a strict cant-sawing structure.

## What's under the hood

A handful of features worth knowing about:

- **Cone compensation.** The big amber banner tells you how much to
  lower the root-side support so the first cut is parallel to the
  pith, not the bark. Turns green once you've cut two reference faces
  180° apart.
- **Edging guide.** Plans the second-pass cuts for any plank that
  came off with wane on one side.
- **Log archive.** Every completed log is saved with its dimensions,
  cuts, produced planks and yield. Up to 50 logs of history, all
  local.
- **English and Swedish.** Switchable from App settings.
- **Per-log wane allowance.** Default zero (strict fit); dial it up
  when you don't mind a corner clip in exchange for a wider plank.
- **Layout responds to your screen.** Single column on a phone,
  side-by-side panels on a tablet, three columns on a wide desktop.
